# AI Second Brain License Policy

## Current status

Version 0.1.7 remains a development prototype. The repository does **not** grant a final open-source or source-available license yet.

The root `LICENSE` file is intentionally marked as pending. Do not assume that source files may be redistributed, relicensed, or incorporated into another product solely because the repository is public.

## Planned release process

Before the first public release, the project owner will select and publish a definitive license. The release will also identify applicable third-party licenses and notices.

## Third-party components

Dependencies, bundled assets, generated artifacts, plugins, SDKs, and external services may carry separate terms. Their licenses must be preserved and documented before redistribution.

## Contributors

Until a contributor agreement or final project license is published, contributors should submit only material they are authorized to contribute and should not include third-party material with incompatible terms.
